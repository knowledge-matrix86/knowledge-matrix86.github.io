Research Method

Knowledge Matrix distinguishes published evidence from analytical synthesis. Original frameworks are explicitly identified and should not be interpreted as claims made by the cited studies themselves.

Where evidence is insufficient, uncertainty and evidence gaps are reported rather than converted into unsupported conclusions.
